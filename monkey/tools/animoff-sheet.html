Backdrop research · part four ·

Do any of them actually walk?

Seven models were given one brief for an eight-frame walk cycle. A still sheet cannot answer whether the result animates — a character that changes height by eight percent between frames looks fine laid out in a row and pulses like a heartbeat once it moves. So here they are moving, on the floor they would stand on, at the size they would be.

Each sheet is sliced live in your browser: the magenta key is removed, the strip is cut into frames equal slices, and each figure is isolated from its neighbours by a flood fill bounded to its own slice. Align feet is the correction the real cutter applies — turn it off to see the bob each sheet has without it. The models disagreed about how many frames to draw, so the frame count is per-bench and adjustable; if a walk looks wrong, try the count next door.
