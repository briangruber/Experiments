The Errant Kipper

A one-room point-and-click adventure, built to find out whether a generated-asset pipeline can carry the genre. The backdrop was repainted from the game's own blockout; the barrel, crates, nets and cup are drawn by code on top of it, because a repaint holds a dock and loses a teacup. Every line is voiced.
