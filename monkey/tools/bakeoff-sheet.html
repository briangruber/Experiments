The same pirate harbour, described once, rendered by six image models in three period hands. Subject text is identical across every tile — only the style block and the model change — so the difference you see is the difference that was tested. Cost is the measured fal balance delta per generation, not a list price.

Total spend is exact — the fal account balance read before the first tile and after the last. The per-tile figures beside each image are balance deltas, which carry roughly one tile of billing lag even with a settle-and-quiesce around each call; treat them as indicative and the total as the real number.
