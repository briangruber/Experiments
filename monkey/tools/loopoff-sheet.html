Which model makes it breathe?

The whole run — eight clips across both stills — cost exactly, read off the fal balance before the first clip and after the last. The per-clip figures are balance deltas and carry about a clip of billing lag, which is why they do not sum to it. Motion is the median inter-frame byte size as a fraction of the keyframe, read straight out of the mp4 sample table: inter-coded frames cost bytes in direct proportion to how much changed, so it measures movement without decoding anything. Under 2% is a held still. It has one real weakness, found the hard way: synthesised in-between frames halve the change from one frame to the next, so a model interpolating to 32 fps scores about half what the same motion scores at 16. Every clip here is therefore generated with frame interpolation off.
