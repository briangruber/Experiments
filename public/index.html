How it works. Everyone is a blob. One blob is holding a live bomb. Touch someone to give it away. When the fuse runs out, whoever is holding it pops. Get shoved off the edge and you're out too. Last blob standing wins.

Dying isn't the end. You come back as a ghost on the rim and can launch spooks at the living — so the blob you eliminated can absolutely still knock you into the void.
