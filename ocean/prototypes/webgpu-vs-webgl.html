WebGPU vs WebGL2, on this machine

Three measurements that decide whether porting Abyssal's renderer to WebGPU would make it faster. Everything runs locally; nothing is uploaded.

And the number that matters most

The benchmark above measures the two halves of the frame in isolation. What decides the answer is how much of a real frame each half actually is. Open the ocean simulator, then paste this into its console — it ablates the simulation and reports its share of the frame on your GPU. Takes ~20 seconds and the picture will freeze partway through, which is expected.
