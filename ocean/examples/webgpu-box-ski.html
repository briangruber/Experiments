Water

Hover is metres the ski sits above the water. 0 is the mass/volume waterline. It is not the motor — W/S still drives. Buoyancy is ρ × volume (1025 kg/m³). The SKI hull only wets the bottom few centimetres (closed foam, not a displacement hull).

How readily the hull leaves a falling face. 0 stays glued to the swell; higher can launch off a crest. Independent of hover.

Hull carve

The hull presses a hollow into the sea and stands the water up as a bow heap and shoulder mounds. Depth is metres of wake-arm / trough relief behind it.

Wake

Leftover gravity waves (wake.physics) plus the foam-energy ribbon. foam is the white film; motor is stern prop wash on craft. Airborne whitewater is spray emitters — not leftover splash particles. Rings / cut / painted V stay off while physics is on.

Swell

The sea dragon's just-under look, on any mesh. Off until you turn swell up. Dome is the loaf on the back, bow is the heap at the nose, mound is the laminar sheet along the body. They peak at the waterline and die when you fly out or dive deep.

Spray

Particles where the mesh cuts the sea. Sites is how many of those cuts emit at once. Band is extra metres of depth that still count as cutting. Hull keeps the ski jet / chine / curtain; off, water sheds from the contact points. Size, hang time and the jet recipe stay on the Sea panel (K).

Mesh

Length rebuilds the mesh. Width and height fatten it. Cyan balls are buoyancy probes; magenta balls are waterline spray cuts; the amber cylinder is the rod on the middle of the back.

Pole cut
