Abyssal · Three.js examples

Serve this directory (npm start) — ES modules will not load from file://.

WebGPU — the whole ocean in one call The createAbyssal() facade: FFT sea, volumetric sky, spray and HDR post, on WebGPU with WebGL2 as the automatic fallback. Presets and real cloud genera as buttons; your meshes share the frame's depth. WebGPU — a sky of real clouds, no water water: false: the atmosphere and cloud raymarch over your own ground. Cirrus, cumulus, stratus, nimbus, cumulonimbus — measured recipes, switchable live, with a sun-elevation slider. Water and sky Both components sharing one parameter set, with Three.js pilings standing in the sea. Watch the waterline move across them — that is a shared depth buffer. Water only The sea under Three's own scene.background. Shows the ordering change a background forces, and what you give up without the matching sky. Sky only Atmosphere and volumetric clouds over a scene with no water at all, with live sun-elevation and cloud-cover controls.
Each page loads Three from a CDN and Abyssal from ../src. Start with the integration guide.
