Harpoon & Hold

A little boat. A very large sea. Something down there is bigger than both.

The work

Sail out, harpoon a monster, hold the rope while it runs, and winch it in. Tow the carcass back to town and sell it.

The ladder

Gold buys a bigger hull. A bigger hull survives further out. Further out is where the real monsters are.

The risk

They bite back. They eat crew. If your hull goes under you wake up in a rowboat with a lighter purse — and they are still out there.
