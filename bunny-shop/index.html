    Hop & Shop

    A very small grocery for very demanding rabbits.

    1. Rabbits hop in, browse, and queue at your counter.
    2. Read the order, then click the crates on the counter to bag their produce.
    3. Ring the brass bell to take their money. Be quick — rabbits are not patient.
    4. Click a rabbit to pet it. This is scientifically calming. Once each.
